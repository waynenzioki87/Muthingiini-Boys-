Our Community

This page highlights the strong partnerships and activities that connect Muthiingini Boys High School with the local community.

Parent-Teacher Association

The PTA supports student welfare, school events and academic programs through active family engagement.

Alumni Network

Former students mentor learners, support school projects and keep the Muthiingini spirit alive.

Local Partnerships

We collaborate with local businesses and nonprofits on community service, internships and outreach.

Environmental Projects

Students engage in tree planting, clean-ups, and environmental stewardship across the area.
